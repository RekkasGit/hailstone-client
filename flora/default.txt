# Hailstone's own flora rules (assets/zone_flora.h, docs/graphics.md "Zone flora"). The shared flora for the outdoor
# classic zones: a zone without a file of its own takes these when the server calls it outdoor. Grass grows on level,
# open ground where the texel under it is green, so sand, snow, rock, roads, water and lava stay bare; each plant
# takes the ground's colour. The shapes and pictures are the player's own commonlands.eqg's (the revamped
# Commonlands), read from the game folder. The first matching line wins.
donor commonlands.eqg
max_slope 45
scale 0.9 1.3
include bare
# Ground named as grass (jungle floors too): its olive and dark grass as well, only its paths and bare patches not.
ground *jung* 12 +greenish grassblades:4 grass2:3 treeshade:1 treeshade2:1
ground *grass* 12 +greenish grassblades:4 grass2:3 treeshade:1 treeshade2:1
ground *gras* 12 +greenish grassblades:4 grass2:3 treeshade:1 treeshade2:1
include stone
# Anything else that is clearly green.
ground * 12 +green grassblades:4 grass2:3 treeshade:1 treeshade2:1
